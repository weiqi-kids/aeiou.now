### summary
巴西的狂歡節不是法定假日。它是「彈性放假日」——由管理部每年發布法令，指定星期一與星期二為聯邦機關的彈性放假日，聖灰星期三則放到下午兩點。也就是說，全世界最大的節慶在它自己的國家沒有法定假日的地位，靠的是一份逐年重發的行政命令。美國把它變成路易斯安那州的法定假日，日本把它移到八月、拿掉了齋戒，印度果阿留下了葡萄牙統治時期的形式。

### customs BR carnaval
巴西的狂歡節在法律上的位置比它的名氣低得多：它**不是法定假日**，而是聯邦機關的「彈性放假日」（ponto facultativo）。管理與公共服務創新部每年發一次法令指定哪幾天，二○二六年是二月十六日星期一與二月十七日星期二，聖灰星期三二月十八日則到下午兩點為止。彈性放假日的意思是各機關可以自行決定要不要停止上班，前提是不影響被認定為必要的服務——所以同一個國家、同一個日子，機關與機關之間可能不一樣。私人企業則完全不受這份法令拘束，是否休假由勞資自行約定。里約與聖保羅的森巴學校遊行是售票的競賽，各州與各市另有自己的地方法規把某些日子訂成市級假日，那是另一層規則。
### customs US mardi-gras
美國沒有全國性的狂歡節。Mardi Gras 是**路易斯安那州的法定假日**，寫在該州修訂法規第一卷第五十五條的公休日條文裡——與元旦、獨立紀念日、勞動節、退伍軍人節、感恩節、聖誕節列在同一份清單上，州政府員工在可行範圍內不上班。同一條條文再往下把權限交給地方：奧爾良、聖伯納、傑佛遜等二十九個堂區以及所有自治市，只要地方議會以條例宣告，Mardi Gras 就是當地的假日。所以「這天放不放假」在美國是逐級決定的——聯邦不放、路易斯安那州放、其他州的市鎮要看有沒有通過那條條例。

### customs JP asakusa-samba
日本的桑巴嘉年華把時間整個搬走了。浅草サンバカーニバル在**八月的最後一個星期六**舉行，與四旬期沒有任何關係——台東區主辦，一九八一年開始，二○二六年是第四十一屆，路線從馬道通到雷門通，近年觀眾約五十萬人。它保留了狂歡節的形式（隊伍、服裝、打擊樂、評分競賽），拿掉了它的曆法理由：在一個沒有四旬期傳統的國家，狂歡節剩下的是遊行本身。這也是北半球規模最大的桑巴嘉年華之一。

## locale en
### title
Carnival
### summary
Carnival is not a public holiday in Brazil. It is a *ponto facultativo* — a discretionary day off that the management ministry designates by decree each year for federal offices, with Ash Wednesday running only until two in the afternoon. The largest festival on earth has no statutory holiday status in its own country; it rests on an administrative order reissued every year. The United States turned it into a Louisiana state holiday, Japan moved it to August and dropped the fast, and Goa kept the form left behind by Portuguese rule.
### keywords
Carnival, Carnaval, Mardi Gras, Lent, Ash Wednesday, Brazil Carnival, Goa Carnival, Asakusa Samba Carnival
### customs BR carnaval
Carnival sits much lower in Brazilian law than its fame suggests: it is **not a public holiday** but a *ponto facultativo*, a discretionary day off for federal bodies. The Ministry of Management and Innovation in Public Services issues a decree once a year naming the days — for 2026, Monday 16 and Tuesday 17 February, with Ash Wednesday on 18 February running only until 2 p.m. Discretionary means each body decides for itself whether to close, provided services deemed essential keep running, so the same day can be worked in one federal office and not in another. Private employers are not bound by the decree at all; whether anyone gets the day is a matter for the employment contract. The samba-school parades in Rio and São Paulo are ticketed competitions, and states and municipalities pass their own laws making certain days local holidays — a separate layer of rule.
### date_rule US mardi-gras
The forty-seventh day before Easter, the day before Ash Wednesday.
### customs US mardi-gras
The United States has no national Carnival. Mardi Gras is a **Louisiana state holiday**, written into R.S. 1:55 among the days of public rest — listed alongside New Year's Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Veterans' Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas, on which state employees are not to work so far as is practicable. The same section then hands the decision downward: in twenty-nine named parishes — Orleans, St. Bernard, Jefferson and the rest — and in all municipalities, Mardi Gras is a holiday when the governing authority declares it so by ordinance. Whether the day is off is therefore decided level by level in the United States: not federally, yes in Louisiana, and elsewhere only where a town has passed that ordinance.
### date_rule IN goa-carnaval
From the fiftieth to the forty-seventh day before Easter — the Saturday to the Tuesday before Ash Wednesday.
### customs IN goa-carnaval
Goa's Carnival is a form left behind by Portuguese rule. The state Department of Tourism runs Viva Carnaval each year, spreading the float parades over four days and four towns — usually Panaji, Margao, Vasco and Mapusa — on the Saturday to Tuesday before Ash Wednesday. It appears in the department's annual state festivals calendar, and every float must have its design and theme approved in advance. Goa is the only Indian state that runs a pre-Lent carnival as an official event, and until 1961 it was the capital of Portuguese India.
### date_rule JP asakusa-samba
The last Saturday in August.
### customs JP asakusa-samba
Japan moved its samba carnival off the calendar entirely. The Asakusa Samba Carnival is held on the **last Saturday in August** and has nothing to do with Lent. Taito Ward runs it; it began in 1981, the 2026 edition is the forty-first, the route goes from Umamichi-dori to Kaminarimon-dori, and recent years have drawn crowds of about half a million. It kept the form of carnival — troupes, costumes, percussion, a scored competition — and discarded its reason for existing on that date: in a country with no Lenten tradition, what is left of carnival is the parade itself. It is among the largest samba carnivals in the northern hemisphere.
### date_rule BR carnaval
From the forty-eighth to the forty-seventh day before Easter — the two days before Ash Wednesday, the first day of Lent.
