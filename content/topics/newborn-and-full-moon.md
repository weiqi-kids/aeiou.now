## locale en
### title
Newborns and the First Month
### summary
Newborn life follows two clocks: birth registration and family celebration. Japan gives 14 days for the birth notification, India 21 days, mainland China one month, Brazil 15 days with a longer period for remote areas, Taiwan and Indonesia generally 60 days, while the US leaves the process to each state. Family customs run on another schedule: Japan has a seventh-day naming and shrine visit, Indonesia has seventh-day aqiqah, and Taiwan and mainland China often mark the first month with a gathering. This guide separates documents, names, blessings, visits, gifts, and early care.

Newborns and the First Month,newborn naming,first-month celebrations,welcoming a baby,global family customs
