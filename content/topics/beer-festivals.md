## locale zh-TW
### title
啤酒節
### summary
同一個從德國搬出去的節慶，各國政府站的位置完全不同。巴西的十月啤酒節由布盧梅瑙市政府主辦，主場館本身就登記在市府的機關表上；中國的青島國際啤酒節由青島市政府主辦，辦在七月到八月而不是十月，源頭是一九〇三年德國與英國商人在青島建的啤酒廠；美國的辛辛那提由民間主辦，政府只負責核發臨時售酒許可，而且明文規定不得發給營利者；印尼則因為酒類管制，同一種活動在制度上不存在。政府自己辦、和政府管到辦不成，是同一條軸的兩端。

### customs BR oktoberfest-blumenau
巴西的十月啤酒節在聖卡塔琳娜州的布盧梅瑙市，二〇二六年是第四十一屆，十月七日到二十五日、連續十九天。它不是民間辦給市府看的活動——主場館「日耳曼村園區」（Parque Vila Germânica）本身就列在市府網站的「政府組織」頁上，和各局處並列，也就是說辦節的單位是市政機關。市府觀光與休閒局的頁面把它寫成「世界上第二大的同名節慶，僅次於慕尼黑的那一個，也是巴西最大的德裔節慶」。二〇二六年的技術節目表有八場官方遊行，七場在市中心的十一月十五日街、一場在維拉伊托帕瓦區。這是七個市場裡唯一一個「市政府是主辦人」的例子。
### customs CN qingdao-beer-festival
中國的版本不在十月。第三十六屆青島國際啤酒節在二〇二六年七月十七日到八月十五日，為期三十天，由青島市政府主辦，西海岸新區管委會、崂山區政府、市南區政府、市北區政府與市歷史城區保護更新指揮部辦公室聯合承辦——承辦名單整排都是行政機關。主題是「青島與世界乾杯」，設西海岸、崂山、青島老城三個會場，三個會場延續免門票入園，匯集兩千三百餘款國內外品牌。它的源頭是一段殖民史：一九〇三年八月十五日，香港盎格魯－日耳曼啤酒公司的德國與英國商人（以德國人為主）在青島米勒上尉路（今登州路五十六號）出資興建日耳曼啤酒公司青島股份公司，設計年產能兩千噸，是中國第一家、也是持續經營至今的啤酒廠。二〇〇六年五月二十五日國務院公布第六批全國重點文物保護單位，「青島啤酒廠早期建築」名列其中。
### customs US oktoberfest-zinzinnati
美國沒有全國性的啤酒節，也沒有任何一級政府主辦。辛辛那提的 Oktoberfest Zinzinnati 二〇二六年在九月十七日到二十日，場地是河岸邊的 Sawyer Point 與 Yeatman's Cove，免入場費；活動自述一九七六年首辦，去年到場人次創下八十萬八千三百人的紀錄，冠名贊助商直接掛在活動名稱上。政府的角色在另一邊：俄亥俄州商務部酒類管理局規定，要在活動現場論杯賣酒或收取含酒入場費，就得先取得臨時許可，法源是《俄亥俄州法典》第 4303.20 至 4303.2012 條。最常見的 F 類許可只准賣啤酒、最長五天、規費四十美元、活動前三十天送件，同一團體三十天內最多兩張；F-2 類可賣啤酒、葡萄酒與烈酒，最長四天、規費一百五十美元，而且只能在地方公投通過為「可售酒」的地區使用。申請人只限慈善、勞工、教育、文化、社會、政治或體育性質的非營利團體，條文明寫「不得為私人的營利或所得核發臨時許可」。同一個活動，在巴西是市政府自己辦，在美國是政府發一張限期執照給非營利團體。
### customs JP yokohama-oktoberfest
日本把這個節慶當成場地活動來辦。橫濱十月啤酒節二〇二六年在九月十八日到十月四日、共十七天，場地是橫濱紅磚倉庫的活動廣場；紅磚倉庫本身帶有德國建築樣式，所以這裡從二〇〇三年開始辦日本版的十月啤酒節，二〇二六年是第二十三回。這一屆比照慕尼黑的做法，德國政府認可的六大釀造廠中有五家參加，二十家以上店鋪進場。它在日本的假日制度裡沒有位置——真正管到它的是稅法：依國稅廳的規定，在博覽會場等地臨時設攤零售酒類，必須向所轄稅務署申請「期限付酒類小售業執照」，原則上要在開設日的兩週前提出，符合一定要件者可在十日前改用申報。要件裡包括會場不得由主辦者自己管理運營、同一人在同一場所每月只能申報一次、活動內容不得以賣酒為主要目的。日本管的是那張限期執照，不是那個節日。

## locale en
### title
Beer Festivals
### summary
The same German festival, exported, and the decisive difference is where the state stands. Brazil's Oktoberfest in Blumenau is run by the city itself — the venue is listed among the municipal organs. China's Qingdao International Beer Festival is run by the municipal government and falls in July and August, not October, tracing back to a brewery German and British merchants built in Qingdao in 1903. In the United States the Cincinnati festival is privately run and the state's only role is a temporary liquor permit that by law may not be issued for private gain. In Indonesia, alcohol control means the event does not exist at all.
### keywords
Oktoberfest, beer festival, Oktoberfest Blumenau, Qingdao International Beer Festival, Oktoberfest Zinzinnati, Yokohama Oktoberfest, liquor permit
### date_rule BR oktoberfest-blumenau
Roughly nineteen consecutive days from early to late October, announced each year by the city of Blumenau.
### customs BR oktoberfest-blumenau
Brazil's Oktoberfest is held in Blumenau, Santa Catarina; the 2026 edition is the forty-first and runs from 7 to 25 October, nineteen days. It is not a private event that the city merely permits: the main site, Parque Vila Germânica, appears on the municipal website's own list of government organs, alongside the secretariats — the body that stages the festival is a body of the city administration. The city's tourism and leisure secretariat describes it as "the second largest version of the festival in the world, second only to its namesake in Munich, and the largest German festival in Brazil". The 2026 programme has eight official parades, seven on Rua 15 de Novembro in the centre and one in the Vila Itoupava district. Of the seven markets on this page, this is the only one where a government is the organiser.
### date_rule CN qingdao-beer-festival
About thirty consecutive days from the third Friday in July, announced each year by the Qingdao municipal government.
### customs CN qingdao-beer-festival
China's version is not in October. The thirty-sixth Qingdao International Beer Festival ran from 17 July to 15 August 2026, thirty days, hosted by the Qingdao municipal government with the West Coast New Area administrative committee and the Laoshan, Shinan and Shibei district governments as co-organisers — the whole co-organiser list is administrative bodies. The theme is "Qingdao toasts the world"; there are three sites (West Coast, Laoshan and the old city), all three keeping free entry, and more than 2,300 Chinese and foreign brands. The origin is colonial: on 15 August 1903, German and British merchants of the Hong Kong Anglo-German Brewery Company (mostly Germans) financed the Germania Brewery Qingdao Co. on Captain Müller Street, now 56 Dengzhou Road, with a designed capacity of 2,000 tonnes a year — the first brewery in China and the only one from that era still operating. On 25 May 2006 the State Council listed the "early buildings of the Tsingtao Brewery" among the sixth batch of Major Historical and Cultural Sites Protected at the National Level.
### date_rule US oktoberfest-zinzinnati
Thursday to Sunday around the third Saturday in September, announced each year by the organiser.
### customs US oktoberfest-zinzinnati
There is no national beer festival in the United States, and no level of government stages one. Oktoberfest Zinzinnati ran 17–20 September 2026 at Sawyer Point and Yeatman's Cove on the Cincinnati riverfront, with free admission; the festival says it was first held in 1976 and drew a record 808,300 people last year, and its title sponsor is written into the event name. Government appears on the other side of the counter. Ohio's Division of Liquor Control requires a temporary permit before anyone sells drinks by the glass or charges admission that includes alcohol, under Ohio Revised Code sections 4303.20 to 4303.2012. The common F permit covers beer by the glass for up to five days, costs $40, must be filed thirty days ahead, and is limited to two per organisation in any thirty-day period; the F-2 permit covers beer, wine and spirits for up to four days at $150 and only in areas voted "wet" locally. Applicants must be charitable, fraternal, labour, educational, cultural, social, political, patriotic or athletic non-profits, and the rule states plainly that no temporary permit may be issued for the profit or gain of a private person. The same festival is a city department in Brazil and a time-limited licence to a non-profit in the United States.
### date_rule JP yokohama-oktoberfest
About seventeen days, from a Friday in mid-September to the first Sunday in October, announced each year by the organiser.
### customs JP yokohama-oktoberfest
Japan treats the festival as a venue event. Yokohama Oktoberfest ran from 18 September to 4 October 2026, seventeen days, on the event plaza of the Yokohama Red Brick Warehouse; because the warehouse borrows elements of German architecture, a Japanese Oktoberfest has been staged there since 2003, and 2026 was the twenty-third edition. Following Munich practice, five of the six breweries recognised by the German government took part, with more than twenty stalls. The event has no place in Japan's holiday law; what actually governs it is tax law. The National Tax Agency requires anyone setting up a temporary retail point to sell alcohol at an exhibition site to obtain a time-limited retail licence from the district tax office, in principle applied for two weeks before opening, or notified ten days ahead where the conditions are met. Those conditions include that the venue is not managed by the organiser itself, that the same party may file only once a month for the same place, and that selling alcohol must not be the main purpose of the event. Japan regulates the licence, not the festival.

### customs US oktoberfest-zinzinnati
Os Estados Unidos não têm uma festa da cerveja nacional, e nenhum nível de governo organiza uma. A Oktoberfest Zinzinnati foi de 17 a 20 de setembro de 2026, no Sawyer Point e no Yeatman's Cove, à beira do rio em Cincinnati, com entrada gratuita; a organização diz que a festa foi realizada pela primeira vez em 1976 e que no ano passado recebeu um recorde de 808.300 pessoas, e o nome do patrocinador principal entra no próprio nome do evento. O poder público aparece do outro lado do balcão. A Divisão de Controle de Bebidas de Ohio exige uma licença temporária antes que alguém venda bebida em copo ou cobre ingresso que inclua álcool, com base nas seções 4303.20 a 4303.2012 do Ohio Revised Code. A licença F, a mais comum, cobre apenas cerveja em copo, dura no máximo cinco dias, custa US$ 40, precisa ser pedida trinta dias antes e é limitada a duas por entidade a cada trinta dias; a licença F-2 cobre cerveja, vinho e destilados por até quatro dias, custa US$ 150 e só vale em áreas em que a venda foi aprovada em consulta local. Só podem pedir entidades sem fins lucrativos de caráter beneficente, fraternal, sindical, educacional, cultural, social, político, patriótico ou esportivo, e a norma diz expressamente que nenhuma licença temporária pode ser emitida para o lucro ou ganho de um particular. A mesma festa é uma repartição municipal no Brasil e uma licença por prazo determinado dada a uma entidade sem fins lucrativos nos Estados Unidos.

O Japão trata a festa como um evento de espaço.
