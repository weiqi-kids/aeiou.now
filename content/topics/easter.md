## locale zh-TW
### title
復活節
### summary
復活節在四個地方的制度位置差距很大，而且方向出乎意料：穆斯林人口占多數的印尼把受難日、復活節、升天節、聖誕節**四個**基督教節日都列為國定假日；基督徒占多數的美國一個都沒有列進聯邦假日；巴西把宗教假日的決定權交給市議會、上限四個；印度列進全國清單的是受難日而不是復活節。
### keywords
復活節、Páscoa、Paskah、Good Friday、受難日、復活節彩蛋
### customs ID paskah
印尼是這四個地方裡把基督教節日納入國定假日最完整的：二○二六年的三部會聯合公告裡，四月三日「耶穌受難」、四月五日「耶穌復活（Paskah）」、五月十四日「耶穌升天」、十二月二十五日「耶穌誕生」全部是國定假日。也就是說，一個穆斯林人口占多數的國家，把整條復活節到升天節的教會曆都放進了全國行事曆。
### customs BR pascoa
巴西的宗教假日不由聯邦決定。《第九○九三號法》第二條規定，宗教性的「守日」由**市級法律**依地方傳統宣告，數量不得超過四個，而聖週五（Sexta-Feira da Paixão）明文包含在這四個之內。所以同一個復活節前後，鄰近兩個市可能一個放假一個不放——宗教節日在巴西是地方自治的事項，不是全國一致的安排。
### customs IN easter
印度的全國假日清單裡有 Good Friday，卻沒有復活節主日。原因很實際：復活節必然落在星期日，而受難日落在星期五，只有後者需要用假日把上班日空出來。這個安排說明國定假日清單解決的是「哪一天要停止工作」，不是「哪一天在宗教上最重要」——兩件事在制度上經常不一致。
### customs US easter-sunday
美國沒有把復活節列為聯邦假日，受難日也不是。聯邦假日清單裡沒有任何一個以基督教節期命名的日子，除了聖誕節。復活節之所以「不需要」放假，部分理由與印度相同：它本來就在星期日。實際的節日內容——教會禮拜、彩蛋、家庭午餐、白宮草坪的滾彩蛋活動——全部發生在既有的週末裡，不佔用行事曆。

## locale en
### title
Easter
### summary
Easter's institutional position differs sharply across these four places, and not in the direction most people expect: Muslim-majority Indonesia lists **four** Christian observances as national holidays — Good Friday, Easter, Ascension and Christmas; the Christian-majority United States lists none of them as federal holidays except Christmas; Brazil hands the decision on religious holidays to municipal councils with a cap of four; and India's national list carries Good Friday rather than Easter Sunday.
### keywords
Easter, Páscoa, Paskah, Good Friday, Ascension, Easter eggs
### customs ID paskah
Indonesia goes furthest of the four in putting Christian observances into the national calendar. In the 2026 joint ministerial decree, 3 April is the death of Jesus Christ, 5 April is the resurrection of Jesus Christ (Paskah), 14 May is the Ascension, and 25 December is the birth of Jesus Christ — all four are national holidays. A country with a Muslim majority has placed the entire stretch of the church calendar from Good Friday to Ascension into the national schedule.
### customs BR pascoa
Brazil's religious holidays are not decided federally. Article 2 of Law 9,093/1995 provides that religious days of observance are declared by **municipal law** according to local tradition, capped at four, and states explicitly that Good Friday is counted among those four. So around the same Easter, two neighbouring municipalities may differ on whether anyone gets the day off — religious holidays in Brazil are a matter of local self-government, not a nationally uniform arrangement.
### customs IN easter
India's national holiday list contains Good Friday but not Easter Sunday. The reason is practical: Easter always falls on a Sunday, while Good Friday falls on a Friday, and only the latter needs a holiday to clear a working day. The arrangement shows that a national holiday list answers the question of which day work stops, not which day matters most religiously — the two frequently fail to line up in institutional terms.
### customs US easter-sunday
The United States does not list Easter as a federal holiday, and Good Friday is not one either. No day named after a Christian observance appears on the federal holiday list except Christmas. Part of why Easter does not 'need' a holiday is the same reason as in India: it already falls on a Sunday. The actual content of the day — church services, eggs, a family lunch, the egg roll on the White House lawn — all happens inside a weekend that already existed, taking nothing from the calendar.

### keywords
復活祭, イースター, Páscoa, Paskah, 聖金曜日
### customs ID paskah

### customs BR pascoa
ブラジルの宗教祝日は連邦が決めない。1995年第9093号法第2条は、宗教上の「守るべき日」は地域の伝統に従い**市の法律**によって宣言され、その数は四を超えてはならないと定め、そこに聖金曜日（Sexta-Feira da Paixão）が含まれると明記している。したがって同じ復活祭の前後でも、隣り合う二つの市で休みになるかどうかが分かれうる。ブラジルにおいて宗教祝日は地方自治の事項であり、全国一律の仕組みではない。
### customs IN easter
インドの全国祝日一覧には聖金曜日があるが、復活祭当日はない。理由は実務的である。復活祭は必ず日曜に当たり、聖金曜日は金曜に当たる。休日にして平日を空ける必要があるのは後者だけだ。この扱いは、国の祝日一覧が答えているのは「どの日に仕事を止めるか」であって「宗教的にどの日が最も重いか」ではないことを示している。制度上、この二つはしばしば一致しない。
### customs US easter-sunday
米国は復活祭を連邦の祝日にしておらず、聖金曜日もそうではない。連邦祝日の一覧に、クリスマスを除いてキリスト教の行事に由来する日は一つもない。復活祭が休日を「必要としない」理由の一つはインドと同じで、もともと日曜だからである。実際の中身——礼拝、卵、家族の昼食、ホワイトハウスの芝生でのエッグロール——はすべて既にある週末の中で起き、暦を消費しない。

### title
Paskah
### summary
Kedudukan kelembagaan Paskah sangat berbeda di empat tempat ini, dan arahnya tidak seperti yang diduga banyak orang: Indonesia yang berpenduduk mayoritas Muslim menetapkan **empat** peringatan Kristen sebagai hari libur nasional — Jumat Agung, Paskah, Kenaikan, dan Natal; Amerika Serikat yang mayoritas Kristen tidak menjadikan satu pun sebagai hari libur federal kecuali Natal; Brasil menyerahkan keputusan hari libur keagamaan kepada dewan kota dengan batas empat; dan daftar nasional India memuat Jumat Agung, bukan Minggu Paskah.
### keywords
Paskah, Jumat Agung, Kenaikan Isa Almasih, Easter, Páscoa
### customs ID paskah
Indonesia paling jauh di antara keempatnya dalam memasukkan peringatan Kristen ke dalam kalender nasional. Dalam SKB tiga menteri 2026, 3 April adalah wafat Yesus Kristus, 5 April kebangkitan Yesus Kristus (Paskah), 14 Mei kenaikan Yesus Kristus, dan 25 Desember kelahiran Yesus Kristus — keempatnya hari libur nasional. Artinya, negara berpenduduk mayoritas Muslim menempatkan seluruh rentang kalender gereja dari Jumat Agung sampai Kenaikan ke dalam jadwal nasional.
### customs BR pascoa
Hari libur keagamaan di Brasil tidak ditentukan di tingkat federal. Pasal 2 Undang-Undang 9.093/1995 menyatakan bahwa hari-hari peringatan keagamaan ditetapkan melalui **undang-undang kota** sesuai tradisi setempat, paling banyak empat, dan menyebut secara tegas bahwa Jumat Agung termasuk di dalam empat itu. Maka di seputar Paskah yang sama, dua kota bertetangga bisa berbeda soal ada tidaknya libur — di Brasil hari libur keagamaan adalah urusan otonomi daerah, bukan pengaturan seragam se-negara.
### customs IN easter
Daftar hari libur nasional India memuat Jumat Agung, tetapi tidak Minggu Paskah. Alasannya praktis: Paskah selalu jatuh pada hari Minggu, sedangkan Jumat Agung jatuh pada hari Jumat, dan hanya yang terakhir memerlukan hari libur untuk mengosongkan hari kerja. Pengaturan ini memperlihatkan bahwa daftar hari libur nasional menjawab pertanyaan hari apa pekerjaan berhenti, bukan hari apa yang paling berat secara keagamaan — dua hal yang kerap tidak berimpit secara kelembagaan.
### customs US easter-sunday
Amerika Serikat tidak menjadikan Paskah hari libur federal, dan Jumat Agung pun tidak. Tidak ada satu pun hari bernama peringatan Kristen dalam daftar hari libur federal selain Natal. Sebagian alasan Paskah tidak 'memerlukan' hari libur sama dengan di India: ia memang sudah jatuh pada hari Minggu. Isi hari itu — ibadah gereja, telur, makan siang keluarga, dan menggelindingkan telur di halaman Gedung Putih — semuanya berlangsung di dalam akhir pekan yang sudah ada, tanpa mengambil apa pun dari kalender.

## locale pt-BR
### title
Páscoa
### summary
A posição institucional da Páscoa varia muito entre estes quatro lugares, e não na direção que a maioria imagina: a Indonésia, de maioria muçulmana, lista **quatro** datas cristãs como feriados nacionais — Sexta-feira Santa, Páscoa, Ascensão e Natal; os Estados Unidos, de maioria cristã, não têm nenhuma delas como feriado federal além do Natal; o Brasil entrega a decisão sobre feriados religiosos às câmaras municipais, com teto de quatro; e a lista nacional da Índia traz a Sexta-feira Santa, não o Domingo de Páscoa.
### keywords
Páscoa, Sexta-feira da Paixão, Paskah, Easter, Lei 9.093
### customs ID paskah
A Indonésia é a que mais longe vai entre as quatro ao colocar datas cristãs no calendário nacional. No decreto interministerial de 2026, 3 de abril é a morte de Jesus Cristo, 5 de abril a ressurreição (Paskah), 14 de maio a Ascensão e 25 de dezembro o nascimento — as quatro são feriados nacionais. Ou seja, um país de maioria muçulmana colocou todo o trecho do calendário da igreja, da Sexta-feira Santa à Ascensão, dentro da agenda nacional.
### customs BR pascoa
No Brasil os feriados religiosos não são decididos pela União. O artigo 2º da Lei 9.093/1995 estabelece que os dias de guarda religiosos são declarados por **lei municipal**, conforme a tradição local, em número não superior a quatro, e diz expressamente que a Sexta-Feira da Paixão está incluída nesses quatro. Assim, em torno da mesma Páscoa, dois municípios vizinhos podem divergir sobre haver ou não folga — feriado religioso no Brasil é matéria de autonomia local, não arranjo nacional uniforme.
### customs IN easter
A lista de feriados nacionais da Índia traz a Sexta-feira Santa, mas não o Domingo de Páscoa. O motivo é prático: a Páscoa cai sempre num domingo, enquanto a Sexta-feira Santa cai numa sexta, e só esta precisa de feriado para liberar um dia útil. O arranjo mostra que uma lista de feriados responde a qual dia o trabalho para, não a qual dia pesa mais religiosamente — no plano institucional, as duas coisas frequentemente não coincidem.
### customs US easter-sunday
Os Estados Unidos não listam a Páscoa como feriado federal, e a Sexta-feira Santa também não. Nenhum dia batizado com uma data cristã aparece na lista de feriados federais além do Natal. Parte da razão de a Páscoa não 'precisar' de feriado é a mesma da Índia: ela já cai num domingo. O conteúdo real do dia — culto, ovos, almoço em família, a rolagem de ovos no gramado da Casa Branca — acontece todo dentro de um fim de semana que já existia, sem tirar nada do calendário.
