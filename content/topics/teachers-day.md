### customs CN teachers-day

## locale en
### title
Teachers' Day
### summary
In 2027, the dates on this page are India on 5 September, mainland China on 10 September, Taiwan on 28 September, Brazil on 15 October, Indonesia on 25 November, and the United States' Teacher Appreciation Week on 3–7 May, with local district dates varying. Only Taiwan makes Teachers' Day a national day off; the other dates are commemorative or school-community observances. The page then explains why each place chose its date.
### keywords
2027 Teachers' Day date, when is Teachers' Day 2027, Teachers' Day, Confucius, Hari Guru, Dia dos Professores, Teacher Appreciation Week 2027
### customs TW teachers-day
Taiwan's Teachers' Day is 28 September, the birthday of Confucius. Article 6 of the Act on Commemorative Days and Holidays lists Children's Day, Tomb-Sweeping Day, Labour Day, the Dragon Boat Festival, Teachers' Day and the Mid-Autumn Festival as each being a one-day holiday — among these six places, only Taiwan's Teachers' Day is a public holiday for everyone. On the same morning the Confucius temples in Tainan and Taipei hold the sacrificial rite, an old-form ceremony running alongside the thank-your-teacher events inside schools.
### customs CN teachers-day
Mainland China's Teachers' Day is 10 September, and its origin is precise: on 11 January 1985 the State Council put a proposal to the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress, and on 21 January the ninth session of the Sixth Standing Committee resolved to make 10 September Teachers' Day each year; 10 September that same year was the first one. It is not a day off, and its form is mainly commendations and school events honouring teachers. The tenth of September was fixed only in 1985, placed in the opening days of the school year so that the first act of the new term would be a salute to teachers. It is a commemorative day rather than a holiday, and classes run as usual — so Teachers' Day in China takes the form of commendations and assemblies inside the school rather than a day off. Unlike India's, which is tied to one person's birthday, this one is tied to the rhythm of the term.
### customs IN teachers-day
India's Teachers' Day is 5 September, the birthday of Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan, its second president, who came from a career as a professor of philosophy. The story told is that when students wanted to celebrate his birthday, he suggested honouring the country's teachers instead. The origin of this holiday is therefore neither religious nor calendrical but a refusal — it turns one person's birthday into a profession's day. Senior students commonly take over classes for the day, with roles deliberately reversed. The fifth of September is that date because it is the birthday of Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan, a professor of philosophy who later became president. The story told is that students wished to celebrate his birthday and he asked them to mark the country's teachers instead. That origin ties India's Teachers' Day to a particular person rather than to a statute or an international observance. In schools the day is often given over to senior students taking the classes, playing the exchange of roles through once.
### customs BR dia-dos-professores
Brazil's Teachers' Day is 15 October, established by Decree 52,682 of 1963. The date comes from the schooling decree of 15 October 1827, which required elementary schools to be set up in towns across the country and is one of the starting points of Brazilian public education. So what Brazil's Teachers' Day commemorates is the day the state began providing schooling, not a particular teacher. It is not a day off, though schools usually adjust the day's timetable.
### customs ID hari-guru
Indonesia's National Teachers' Day is 25 November, established by Presidential Decree No. 78 of 1994. The date comes from 25 November 1945, when teachers' organisations across the newly independent country merged to found the Indonesian Teachers' Association. The decree also states plainly that the day is **not a public holiday**. Schools usually mark it with a flag ceremony, awards, and appreciation events organised by the students themselves.
### customs US teacher-appreciation
The United States has no national Teachers' Day; it has Teacher Appreciation Week in the first full week of May, with the actual dates arranged by each school district. Its form sits at the level of the school community: parent associations, students and staff each prepare something, rather than a national commemoration. UNESCO separately marks World Teachers' Day on 5 October, but that is a different thing from the week in the American school calendar.

The first full week of May; local school district dates may vary
