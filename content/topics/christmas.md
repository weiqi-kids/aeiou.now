## locale zh-TW
### title
聖誕節
### summary
十二月二十五日在七個地方不是同一件事：巴西、美國、印尼、印度把它列為法定假日，台灣同一天放的是行憲紀念日，日本與中國大陸則照常上班。這一頁比較它在各地的法律地位、日期前後的作息，以及家庭與商業各自的參與方式。
### keywords
聖誕節、聖誕、Christmas、行憲紀念日、Natal
### customs BR christmas
巴西的十二月二十五日由《六六二號法》列為全國假日。南半球的十二月是夏天，所以節期會把裝飾燈、深夜的家庭晚餐、彌撒和戶外聚會放在一起。城市廣場的大型布置和每個家庭的做法不會長得一樣，有人重視教會，有人重視餐桌。
### customs US christmas
美國把聖誕節列為聯邦假日，公部門與多數企業當天休息。燈飾會出現在住家、商店和整條公共街區，也可能和禮拜、聚餐或送禮一起出現。有人整個十二月都在布置，有人只吃一頓飯，也有家庭完全不過這個節日。
### customs TW constitution-day
台灣的十二月二十五日在法律上是行憲紀念日，依《紀念日及節日實施條例》放假一日；聖誕節本身不是國定節日。同一天裡，教會禮拜、商圈活動和學校的交換禮物各自進行。放假的理由和多數人實際在做的事並不是同一件，這個落差正是台灣的特點。值得記一筆的是這一天的假期地位是變動的：十二月二十五日在二○○一年之後有很長一段時間不放假，直到二○二五年公布的《紀念日及節日實施條例》才把行憲紀念日重新列入放假一日的紀念日。所以「台灣的十二月二十五日放不放假」，答案取決於你問的是哪一年。
### customs JP christmas
日本的聖誕節不放假，二十六日照常上班上學。它更接近情侶與朋友的節日：預訂蛋糕、買炸雞、看街區燈飾。總務省統計局的家計調查顯示，十二月有超過一半的家庭買了蛋糕，而十二月的蛋糕支出以二十四日最多——也就是說，日本的聖誕節高峰在平安夜而不是二十五日。家庭團聚則留給跨年那幾天，禮物交換不是核心。整個十二月在日本也是全年家計支出最高的月份：總務省統計局的資料顯示，該年十二月的每月消費支出為三十一萬八千餘日圓，比全年月平均高出一成以上，第二高的三月都還差一截。忘年會、聖誕、除夕與過年準備擠在同一個月，是這個高峰的成因。
### customs CN christmas
中國大陸的《全國年節及紀念日放假辦法》沒有把聖誕節列入全體公民放假的節日，十二月二十五日是工作日。城市的商場與餐廳會做節期企劃，教堂也有禮拜，但那是城市消費與信仰各自的活動，不是全國性的節慶安排。要看清楚它的位置，可以把放假辦法列的全體公民放假節日攤開來看：元旦、春節、清明節、勞動節、端午節、中秋節、國慶節——七個之中沒有任何一個是宗教節日。聖誕節不在其中，不是被排除，而是這份清單本來就不收宗教曆的日子。
### customs ID natal
印尼把十二月二十五日的「耶穌誕辰」列為國定假日，並常在前後安排共同休假（cuti bersama）。基督徒社群上教堂、家庭聚餐、返鄉探親；在穆斯林為多數的地區同樣放假。節日的法律地位與個人的宗教身分不必然重疊。
### customs IN christmas

## locale en
### title
Christmas
### summary
December 25 is not the same thing in seven places. Brazil, the United States, Indonesia, and India list it as a statutory holiday; Taiwan closes that day for Constitution Day instead; Japan and mainland China treat it as a working day. This page compares its legal standing, the rhythm of the days around it, and how households and commerce each take part.
### keywords
Christmas, Christmas Day, Natal, Constitution Day, public holiday
### customs BR christmas
In Brazil, December 25 is a national holiday under Law 662. December falls in summer south of the equator, so the season can combine decorative lights, a late family supper, Mass, and outdoor gatherings. A city square and a household may mark it in very different ways, some around church and some around the table.
### customs US christmas
Christmas Day is a federal holiday in the United States, and most public offices and businesses close. Lights appear on houses, shops, and whole public streets, sometimes alongside worship, a shared meal, or gifts. Some households decorate all December, some cook one dinner, and some do not observe Christmas at all.
### customs TW constitution-day
In Taiwan, December 25 is legally Constitution Day, a paid public holiday under the Act on Commemorative Days and Holidays; Christmas itself is not a statutory holiday. Church services, shopping-district events, and school gift exchanges all happen on that same day. The reason for the day off and what most people actually do are two different things.Worth noting is that the day's holiday status has moved: after 2001, 25 December went for a long stretch without being a day off, until the Act on Commemorative Days and Holidays promulgated in 2025 put Constitution Day back among the commemorative days that carry one day of leave. So the answer to 'is 25 December a holiday in Taiwan' depends on which year you are asking about.
### customs JP christmas
Christmas is not a public holiday in Japan, and 26 December is an ordinary working day. It reads more like an occasion for couples and friends: a reserved cake, fried chicken, a walk past the illuminations. The Statistics Bureau's household survey shows that more than half of households buy cake in December, and that December cake spending peaks on the 24th — so the Japanese Christmas peak is Christmas Eve, not the 25th. Family gathering belongs to the new-year days instead, and gift exchange is not the centre.December as a whole is also the highest-spending month of the year for Japanese households: the Statistics Bureau's figures put monthly consumption expenditure that December at just over 318,000 yen, more than ten per cent above the annual monthly average, with the second-highest month, March, still some way behind. Year-end parties, Christmas, New Year's Eve and the preparations for the new year all crowd into the same month, which is what produces the peak.
### customs CN christmas
Mainland China's national holiday regulation does not list Christmas among the days off for all citizens, so December 25 is a working day. Shopping centres and restaurants run seasonal campaigns and churches hold services, but that is urban commerce and religious practice acting separately, not a nationwide holiday arrangement.To see where it sits, it helps to lay out the days the regulation gives to all citizens: New Year's Day, Spring Festival, Qingming, Labour Day, Duanwu, Mid-Autumn and National Day — not one of the seven is a religious festival. Christmas is not on the list, not because it was excluded, but because the list does not take days from a religious calendar in the first place.
### customs ID natal
Indonesia lists the Birth of Jesus Christ on December 25 as a national holiday, often with joint leave (cuti bersama) around it. Christian communities attend church, share family meals, and travel home; regions with a Muslim majority take the same day off. The legal standing of the holiday and a person's own faith do not have to line up.
### customs IN christmas
India includes Christmas Day in its list of national holidays. Where the Christian share of the population is larger, midnight Mass, lights, and festive sweets are more visible; elsewhere it may simply be a day off. The weight of the same holiday differs sharply between communities and regions.

### customs JP christmas
日本のクリスマスは休日ではなく、26日は通常の勤務日・登校日である。恋人や友人と過ごす日に近く、予約したケーキ、フライドチキン、街のイルミネーションが中心になる。総務省統計局の家計調査によれば、12月にケーキを購入した世帯の割合は半数以上で、12月のケーキへの支出は24日が最も多い。つまり日本のクリスマスの山は25日ではなく24日にある。家族が集まるのは年末年始で、贈り物のやり取りは主役ではない。12月は日本の家計にとって年間で最も支出の多い月でもある。総務省統計局の資料によれば、その年の12月の消費支出は318,488円で、年平均の282,188円と比べて112.9％、1割以上多い。2番目に多い3月の300,889円とも差がある。忘年会、クリスマス、大みそか、正月の準備が同じ月に重なることが、この山を作っている。
### customs CN christmas

### customs ID natal
インドネシアは12月25日の「イエス・キリスト降誕」を国の祝日とし、前後に共同休暇（cuti bersama）が置かれることも多い。キリスト教徒は教会に行き、家族で食卓を囲み、帰省する。ムスリムが多数の地域でも同じ日が休みになる。祝日の法的地位と個人の信仰は必ずしも一致しない。
### customs IN christmas
