## locale zh-TW
### title
環境紀念日
### summary
環境紀念日幾乎都不放假，七個國家裡只有日本例外——五月四日的みどりの日是《國民の祝日に関する法律》第二條寫明的法定祝日。而它的來歷與環境無關：那一天原本是四月二十九日的天皇誕生日，昭和天皇過世後，一九八九年修法把它改名為みどりの日留住了假期，二〇〇七年再把名字搬到五月四日。其餘六國把環境日寫進法律、政令或人大決定，然後在同一部法裡明文不放假。
### keywords
環境紀念日, 植樹節, 環境日, 世界環境日, みどりの日, 綠之日, 地球日, Earth Day, 全國生態日, 樹木日
### customs JP midori-no-hi
みどりの日（綠之日）是日本十六個法定祝日之一，寫在《國民の祝日に関する法律》（昭和二十三年法律第一七八號）第二條：五月四日，趣旨是「親近自然、感謝自然的恩惠、涵養豐富的心」。同法第三條的一句話造成了它與其他六國的差別——「國民の祝日は、休日とする」，祝日就是假日。但它的來歷跟環境沒有關係：制定當初的天皇誕生日是四月二十九日，也就是昭和天皇的生日；一九八九年（平成元年）皇位繼承後，天皇誕生日改到十二月二十三日，四月二十九日則在同年修法時改名為みどりの日保留下來。二〇〇五年（平成十七年）再修法，二〇〇七年起四月二十九日改為昭和の日，みどりの日移到黃金週當中的五月四日。內閣府另外設了四月十五日到五月十四日的「みどりの月間」與「みどりの學術賞」。
### customs JP kankyo-no-hi
同一個國家的另一個環境日就不放假。環境の日在六月五日，出處是《環境基本法》（平成五年法律第九十一號）第十條：為了讓事業者與國民加深對環境保全的關心與理解、提高從事保全活動的意願而設環境の日；第二項寫明日期是六月五日；第三項只要求國與地方公共團體「努力實施符合該日趣旨的事業」——努力義務，不是假日。環境省把含這一天的六月訂為「環境月間」，令和八年度的取組由大臣官房在二〇二六年五月二十八日發布。日期本身也值得記一筆：它紀念一九七二年六月五日在斯德哥爾摩開幕的聯合國人類環境會議，而聯合國把六月五日訂為世界環境日，是採納日本的提案。台灣、印尼與巴西後來各自使用的那個六月五日，源頭在這裡。
### customs US earth-day
美國的地球日在四月二十二日，環保署（EPA）的官方頁面就是這樣寫的。它不是聯邦假日：《美國法典》第五編第六一〇三條列出的法定公共假日只有十一個——元旦、金恩紀念日、華盛頓誕辰、陣亡將士紀念日、六月節、獨立紀念日、勞動節、哥倫布日、退伍軍人節、感恩節、聖誕節——沒有地球日。更能說明位置的是第三十六編第一章：那份由國會指定的全國紀念日與紀念週清單收了四十八項（第一〇一條到第一四八條），地球日同樣不在裡面；在裡面的環境相關項目是第一〇四條的「聯邦土地清理日」（勞動節後第一個星期六）與第一二三條的「全國林產品週」（十月第三個星期日起那一週）。地球日的來歷是一九七〇年春天由參議員蓋洛德・尼爾森發起，兩千萬美國人走上街頭，同年十二月國會授權設立環保署。
### customs CN zhishu-jie
中國的植樹節寫在法律本體裡。《中華人民共和國森林法》第十條：「植樹造林、保護森林，是公民應盡的義務。各級人民政府應當組織開展全民義務植樹活動。每年三月十二日為植樹節。」這一版森林法由第十三屆全國人大常委會第十五次會議於二〇一九年十二月二十八日修訂通過，自二〇二〇年七月一日起施行。條文把植樹寫成公民義務，而不是假日：依二〇二四年十一月修訂的《全國年節及紀念日放假辦法》，全體公民放假的節日是元旦、春節、清明節、勞動節、端午節、中秋節、國慶節七個，沒有任何環境類的日子。順帶一提，三月十二日在台灣的《紀念日及節日實施條例》裡是國父逝世紀念日——海峽兩岸的植樹節與那個紀念日落在同一天。
### customs CN quanguo-shengtai-ri
全國生態日是這一頁上最新的一個。二〇二三年六月二十八日，第十四屆全國人大常委會第三次會議通過決定，將八月十五日設立為全國生態日，並規定國家通過多種形式開展生態文明宣傳教育活動。它由人大常委會以「決定」設立，法律位階與寫進森林法條文的植樹節不同，但同樣不放假。日期的選法有明白講出來的理由：生態環境部的說明指出，二〇〇五年八月十五日習近平在浙江湖州安吉考察時首次提出「綠水青山就是金山銀山」。首個全國生態日的主場活動在二〇二三年八月十五日於浙江省湖州市舉行，由國家發展改革委聯合中央宣傳部、自然資源部、生態環境部等部門與浙江省人民政府共同舉辦，主題就是那一句話。
### customs TW zhishu-jie
台灣的植樹節在三月十二日，出處是《紀念日及節日實施條例》第五條第六款——它被列為「節日」，不是「紀念日」，兩者在同一部條例裡走不同的放假規則。第六條把放假的節日逐項列出：除夕與春節、兒童節、清明節、勞動節、端午節、教師節、中秋節、原住民族歲時祭儀，以及依主管機關規定放假的消防節、警察節、軍人節、海巡節；末尾一句「第一項以外之節日，均不放假」——植樹節就是被這一句排除的。第七條給它的是另一種待遇：節日由「各機關、團體及學校得依其性質，舉行紀念、慶祝或民俗等相關活動」。還有一件事要讀條文才看得到：同一部條例第三條把三月十二日同時列為國父逝世紀念日，而第四條規定只有六個紀念日放假一日，那一個也不在其中。三月十二日在台灣是一天兩個名字，兩個都不放假。
### customs TW huanjing-ri
六月五日在台灣的法定名稱是「環境日」，寫在《紀念日及節日實施條例》第五條第十四款，本條例的主管機關是內政部（第二條）。它與植樹節適用同一條放假規則，也就是第六條末句的「第一項以外之節日，均不放假」。條文用的是「環境日」三個字，不是日常講的「世界環境日」；而《環境教育法》（民國一〇六年十一月二十九日修正）從第一條到第二十六條都沒有指定任何日期，它管的是環境教育計畫、環境教育設施場所認證與違規者的環境講習時數。所以台灣的六月五日是節日條例給的，不是環境教育法給的——同一個國家裡，教育的義務與日期的宣示分別放在兩部法。
### customs IN pollution-prevention-day
印度的十二月二日不紀念自然，紀念一場事故。喀拉拉邦環境與氣候變遷廳的說明寫得很直接：十二月二日是全國防治污染日（National Pollution Prevention Day，政府網頁上也寫成 National Pollution Control Day），為紀念在博帕爾毒氣事件中喪生的人而設；事件發生在一九八四年十二月二日到三日的夜裡，毒氣是異氰酸甲酯（MIC）。化學品與石化部的官方頁面接著記載了後續的制度：一九八五年二月二十日《博帕爾毒氣洩漏災難（求償處理）法》連同求償登記與處理辦法生效，同年設立博帕爾毒氣受害者福利專員辦公室；一九八九年二月，聯合碳化公司依最高法院指示存入四億七千萬美元賠償金；截至二〇二四年七月三十一日，五十七萬四千三百九十四件求償中已對五十七萬三千九百五十九件發放，共一千五百四十九點三三億盧比。這一天不放假：二〇二六年中央政府機關的公休日清單（依人事培訓部 F.No.12/2/2023-JCA 號公文，二〇二五年七月三日）共十七天，十二月二日不在其中。
### customs ID hari-lingkungan-hidup
印尼的世界環境日（Hari Lingkungan Hidup Sedunia）在六月五日，林業部的官方行事曆就是這樣標的。它不在國定假日清單上：二〇二四年一月二十九日總統簽署的第八號總統決定（Keppres Nomor 8 Tahun 2024）把原本散在多份總統決定裡的假日規定整合成一份，該年列出十六個假日——西曆新年、夜行登霄、農曆新年、寧靜日、耶穌受難日、復活節、開齋節兩日、國際勞動節、耶穌升天、衛塞節、潘查希拉誕生日、宰牲節、伊斯蘭新年、獨立日、先知誕辰、聖誕節——沒有任何一個環境日。印尼另有一個自己訂的日子：十一月二十八日的印尼植樹日（Hari Menanam Pohon Indonesia），同樣列在林業部的行事曆上，同樣不放假。
### customs BR dia-da-arvore
巴西的樹木日有兩個日期，而且它在法律上已經被改掉名字了。一九六五年二月二十四日的第五五七九五號法令（Decreto nº 55.795）第一條寫著：在全國設立「年度樹木節」（Festa Anual das Árvores），以取代「目前在九月二十一日慶祝的所謂『樹木日』」。第三條說明為什麼要拆成兩個日期——巴西的地理與氣候差異太大：阿克里、亞馬孫、帕拉、馬拉尼昂、皮奧伊、塞阿拉、北大河、帕拉伊巴、伯南布哥、阿拉戈斯、塞爾希培與巴伊亞在三月的最後一週慶祝，聖埃斯皮里圖、里約、米納斯吉拉斯、戈亞斯、馬托格羅索、聖保羅、巴拉那、聖卡塔琳娜、南大河與聯邦特區則從九月二十一日那一週開始。第四條把主辦交給農業部與教育文化部。六十年後，巴西人日常講的仍然是那個被取代掉的名字 Dia da Árvore。它不是全國假日：一九四九年第六六二號法律（經二〇〇二年第一〇六〇七號法律修正）列出的全國假日只有一月一日、四月二十一日、五月一日、九月七日、十一月二日、十一月十五日與十二月二十五日。巴西真正用法律訂出來的環境日是另一個——二〇一二年五月十四日第一二六三三號法律設立全國環境教育日，六月三日，全國適用，同樣不放假。

## locale en
### title
Environment Days
### summary
Environment days almost never come with a day off. Across these seven countries there is exactly one exception, and it exists by accident: 4 May in Japan, Midori no Hi, is a statutory public holiday under article 2 of the Act on National Holidays. It began as the Showa Emperor's birthday on 29 April; when the throne passed in 1989 the birthday moved to December and the empty 29 April was renamed Greenery Day so the day off would survive, then shifted again to 4 May in 2007. Everywhere else the environment day is written into a statute, a decree or an assembly resolution — and the same instrument says nobody stops working.
### keywords
Earth Day, Midori no Hi, Greenery Day, Arbor Day, World Environment Day, National Pollution Prevention Day, Dia da Árvore, environment holidays, tree planting day
### customs JP midori-no-hi
Midori no Hi — Greenery Day — is one of Japan's sixteen national holidays, set out in article 2 of the Act on National Holidays (Act No. 178 of 1948): 4 May, for "becoming familiar with nature, being grateful for its blessings, and cultivating a rich heart." Article 3 is the sentence that separates Japan from the other six countries here: national holidays are days off, full stop. The origin, though, has nothing to do with nature. When the Act was written, the Emperor's Birthday fell on 29 April, the birthday of the Showa Emperor. After the accession in 1989 the Emperor's Birthday moved to 23 December, and the 29 April slot was renamed Midori no Hi in that year's amendment so that the holiday itself would remain. A further amendment in 2005 turned 29 April into Showa Day from 2007 and moved Midori no Hi to 4 May, inside Golden Week. The Cabinet Office also runs a Greenery Month from 15 April to 14 May and a Midori Academic Prize.
### customs JP kankyo-no-hi
Japan's other environment day is not a holiday at all. Environment Day falls on 5 June under article 10 of the Basic Environment Act (Act No. 91 of 1993): the day exists to deepen public and business awareness of environmental conservation, paragraph 2 fixes the date, and paragraph 3 asks only that national and local government "endeavour to carry out projects befitting its purpose" — a duty to try, not a day off. The Ministry of the Environment designates the whole of June as Environment Month; the FY2026 programme was announced by the Minister's Secretariat on 28 May 2026. The date carries a detail worth keeping: it marks the UN Conference on the Human Environment that opened in Stockholm on 5 June 1972, and the United Nations designated 5 June as World Environment Day on a proposal from Japan. The 5 June that Taiwan, Indonesia and Brazil each use later on starts here.
### customs US earth-day
Earth Day in the United States is 22 April, as the Environmental Protection Agency's own page states. It is not a federal holiday: 5 U.S.C. §6103 lists eleven legal public holidays — New Year's Day, the Birthday of Martin Luther King Jr., Washington's Birthday, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas — and Earth Day is not among them. The sharper measure is chapter 1 of title 36, the permanent list of patriotic and national observances Congress has designated: forty-eight of them, sections 101 through 148, and Earth Day is absent there too. What is present is §104, Carl Garner Federal Lands Cleanup Day (the first Saturday after Labor Day), and §123, National Forest Products Week (the week beginning the third Sunday in October). Earth Day itself began in the spring of 1970, created by Senator Gaylord Nelson; twenty million Americans demonstrated, and in December of that year Congress authorised the creation of the EPA.
### customs CN zhishu-jie
China put its tree-planting day in the body of a statute. Article 10 of the Forest Law reads: planting trees and protecting forests is a duty of every citizen; governments at every level shall organise the nationwide compulsory tree-planting activity; 12 March each year is Arbor Day. This version of the Forest Law was revised by the fifteenth session of the Standing Committee of the 13th National People's Congress on 28 December 2019 and took effect on 1 July 2020. The wording makes planting an obligation rather than a holiday: under the Measures on Holidays for National Annual Festivals and Memorial Days as revised in November 2024, the days on which all citizens are off work are New Year's Day, Spring Festival, Qingming, Labour Day, Dragon Boat, Mid-Autumn and National Day — seven, none of them environmental. One coincidence is worth noting: 12 March appears in Taiwan's Act on Commemorative Days and Holidays as the anniversary of the death of the founding father, so the two Arbor Days on either side of the strait share a date with that memorial.
### customs CN quanguo-shengtai-ri
National Ecology Day is the newest entry on this page. On 28 June 2023 the third session of the Standing Committee of the 14th National People's Congress adopted a decision establishing 15 August as National Ecology Day, with the state to conduct education and publicity on ecological civilisation in various forms. It was created by a decision of the Standing Committee rather than by an article of a statute, a different rung from Arbor Day, and like Arbor Day it is not a day off. The choice of date was stated openly: the Ministry of Ecology and Environment explained that on 15 August 2005, during an inspection of Anji county in Huzhou, Zhejiang, Xi Jinping first put forward the formulation "lucid waters and lush mountains are invaluable assets." The first National Ecology Day was marked on 15 August 2023 in Huzhou, hosted jointly by the National Development and Reform Commission with the Central Publicity Department, the Ministry of Natural Resources, the Ministry of Ecology and Environment and the Zhejiang provincial government.
### customs TW zhishu-jie
Taiwan's Arbor Day is 12 March, listed in article 5, item 6 of the Act on Commemorative Days and Holidays — as a "festival day", not a "commemorative day", and the two categories run through different rules in the same statute. Article 6 names the festival days that are off work: Lunar New Year's Eve and New Year, Children's Day, Qingming, Labour Day, Dragon Boat, Teachers' Day, Mid-Autumn, indigenous ceremonial days, and the fire service, police, armed forces and coast guard days as their competent authorities decide. It then closes with a single sentence: festival days other than those in the first paragraph are not days off. That sentence is what excludes Arbor Day. Article 7 gives it a different kind of standing instead: agencies, organisations and schools may hold commemorative, celebratory or folk activities as suits the day.
