## locale zh-TW
### title
購物節
### summary
購物節是這些主題裡唯一由零售業自己造出來、政府事後才追認或管制的日子。美國聯邦法裡沒有黑色星期五，只有感恩節；中國的雙十一由市場監管總局每年發合規提示，還把折扣基準寫死成「促銷前七日內最低成交價」；印尼的 Harbolnas 由貿易部掛名，部長本人到電商辦公室直播賣貨；印度不問折扣真不真，改問介面騙不騙人。同一天買同一件東西，能不能退、退幾天、運費誰付，答案在七個市場完全不同。
### keywords
黑色星期五, Black Friday, 雙十一, 網購星期一, Cyber Monday, 双十一, Harbolnas, 購物節, 週年慶, 折扣
### customs US black-friday
美國聯邦法律裡沒有「黑色星期五」這個日子。有法律地位的是前一天的感恩節，列在美國法典第五編第六一〇三條的聯邦假日清單裡；人事管理局公布的行事曆是二〇二六年十一月二十六日、二〇二七年十一月二十五日、二〇二八年十一月二十三日，星期四。翌日那個星期五對聯邦雇員而言是普通上班日。真正管到折扣的是聯邦貿易委員會的《不實定價指引》（聯邦行政命令彙編第十六編第二三三部），一九六七年十一月八日發布後幾乎沒有再改：第二三三·一條說，拿「原價」來比較，那個原價必須是「實際、真誠、以常態方式向大眾提供了相當一段期間的價格」；如果是「為了之後能宣告一個大幅降價而刻意架起來的人造高價」，那個折扣就是假的。要注意它叫「指引」而不是「規則」——它是對聯邦貿易委員會法第五條的解釋，沒有規定回溯幾天。二〇二五年一月十日生效的新規則（第十六編第四六四部）確實要求把總價一次講清楚，但它的適用範圍寫死只有現場活動門票與短期住宿，零售商品不在裡面。
### customs US cyber-monday
網購星期一是感恩節之後的第一個星期一：二〇二六年十一月三十日、二〇二七年十一月二十九日、二〇二八年十一月二十七日。它同樣不在聯邦假日清單裡，聯邦法規也沒有為它寫過任何一條規定——這個名字一開始是零售業公會造出來的行銷詞。值得注意的是連統計都沒有：普查局的電子商務零售額是按季發布的，從來不出單日數字，所以每年新聞上那個「網購星期一破紀錄」的金額全部來自民間業者的估算，不是官方統計。政府在這一天出面的方式是刑事的：聯邦調查局的網路犯罪申訴中心每年感恩節前發布購物季詐騙公告，二〇二三年十一月十五日那一則（編號 I-111523-PSA）指出，光是二〇二二年購物季就收到將近一萬兩千件不付款／不出貨的申訴，損失超過七千三百萬美元。
### customs BR black-friday
巴西的黑色星期五在十一月的最後一個星期五：二〇二六年十一月二十七日、二〇二七年十一月二十六日、二〇二八年十一月二十四日。它不是任何一種假日，也沒有專屬法規，全部靠《消費者保護法》（一九九〇年第八〇七八號法）撐著——第三十七條禁止引人錯誤的廣告，第四十九條給在店外成交（也就是網購、電話下單）的消費者七天反悔權，從收到商品起算、不必說明理由，第二十六條則規定法定保固在打折商品上一樣有效，非耐久財三十天、耐久財九十天。執行的是各州與聯邦特區的消費者保護機關：聯邦特區消費者保護局（隸屬司法與公民權秘書處）在二〇二三年十月宣布加強稽查主要購物中心與最大的幾個電商網站，查的是有沒有履行廣告的優惠、有沒有不實廣告、有沒有客服管道，申訴走十個實體服務據點或 151@procon.df.gov.br；局長 Marcelo Nascimento 當時給消費者的建議是先盯價格、把廣告截圖存下來。該局二〇二五年的提醒還記錄了形狀的改變：促銷已經拉長到整個十一月，也早就跨出零售業——線上課程、諮詢服務、數位商品都在打折，連個人與非正式攤商都在打廣告。「前一天調漲、當天降價」在巴西被明確定性為引人錯誤的廣告，是可以裁罰的。
### customs CN double-eleven
中國的雙十一在十一月十一日，二〇〇九年由電商自己造出來，現在每年由監管機關發文。國家市場監督管理總局逐年向主要平台發布《「雙十一」網路集中促銷合規提示》，二〇二五年十一月十日那一份分六個方面：落實主體責任、規範促銷行為（杜絕「二選一」與「大數據殺熟」）、規範價格行為（不得先提價後打折、不得虛假折價比價）、規範直播行銷、加強廣告審核、及時化解消費糾紛（七日無理由退貨與「三包」）。真正有拘束力的是《規範促銷行為暫行規定》（市場監管總局令第三十二號，二〇二〇年十月二十九日公布，同年十二月一日施行）第二十一條：折價、減價必須標明基準；沒有標明的，基準就是同一經營者在同一經營場所、本次促銷前七日內的最低成交價，前七日內沒有交易的話就以最後一次交易價為準。退貨則來自《消費者權益保護法》第二十五條與《網路購買商品七日無理由退貨暫行辦法》：收到商品起七日內無理由退貨，只排除四類——訂做商品、鮮活易腐、線上下載或拆封的數位商品，以及已交付的報紙期刊。七個市場裡，只有中國把折扣的參考價寫成一個具體天數。
### customs JP black-friday
日本的ブラックフライデー是零售業自己引進的，沒有法律地位，也沒有官方日期——每一家連鎖店在十一月下旬自己公告自己的檔期，長短各不相同。管到價格標示的是《景品表示法》：《關於不當價格表示的景品表示法上的看法》由公正交易委員會二〇〇〇年六月三十日發布，二〇〇二年十二月五日與二〇〇六年一月四日兩次修訂，二〇一六年四月一日移交消費者廳；它說拿過去的售價當比較對照價格，前提是同一商品「在最近相當期間持續以該價格販售過」，而且這個期間不必連續，斷斷續續辦特賣時要整體評價；「販售過」指的是正常營業中有在賣，不要求真的有人買。二〇二〇年十二月二十五日消費者廳另外發布了《以未來售價作為比較對照價格之二重價格表示的執法方針》，針對的是「現在便宜、下週起漲回某價」這種寫法。退貨這一面日本走的路完全不同：通信販售沒有猶豫期制度，依《特定商取引法》第十五條之三，收到商品起八日內可以撤回，但那只在賣方沒有標示自己的退貨特約時才適用——標示了就依它的條件，而且運費由消費者負擔。法律的做法是把退貨條件變成廣告裡不得省略的事項，網購還必須出現在最終確認畫面上。同樣是「七、八天」，日本的是可以被賣方契約排除的預設值。
### customs ID harbolnas
印尼的 Harbolnas（Hari Belanja Online Nasional，全國網購日）在十二月十二日，取自 12.12 這個好記的數字。它二〇一二年由電商平台發起，現在由貿易部掛名參與——七個市場裡唯一由中央政府直接背書的購物節。貿易部把它包裝成「Beli Lokal 12.12」（買在地 12.12）活動，主打中小微企業（UMKM）的商品；二〇二三年的 Harbolnas 辦在十二月十日到十二日，貿易部公開設定的交易額目標是二十五兆印尼盾。二〇二四年十二月十二日，貿易部長 Budi Santoso 本人到 Shopee 位於雅加達的辦公室，直播販售中小微企業的產品，同一檔期在印尼各電商平台同步舉行。法制上的框架是二〇一九年第八十號政府條例《電子系統貿易》。這裡的政府動機和其他市場相反：印尼政府出面不是為了查折扣真假，而是為了把國內產品的銷售與線上出口推上去。
### customs TW double-eleven
台灣的雙十一在十一月十一日，旁邊還有百貨業從九月排到十一月的週年慶，以及近年引進的黑色星期五。三者都沒有任何法律地位，撐著它們的是兩部法。第一部是《公平交易法》第二十一條，禁止事業對商品的價格等事項為虛偽不實或引人錯誤的表示，由公平交易委員會執行——但它沒有像中國那樣規定「原價」要回溯幾天，一則折扣廣告是不是假的，是個案認定。第二部是《消費者保護法》第十九條，也是台灣寫得最硬的一條：通訊交易的消費者得於收受商品後七日內，以退回商品或書面通知解除契約，**無須說明理由及負擔任何費用或對價**；企業經營者在交付時沒有提供解約資訊的話，七日從補提供的次日起算，但收受後逾四個月解除權消滅；而且違反本條的約定一律無效。與日本的差別就在最後那一句——在台灣，賣方不能用自訂的退貨條款把這七天換掉。
### customs IN big-billion-days
印度的節慶檔期沒有官方日期：Big Billion Days 與 Great Indian Festival 都是電商平台自己的檔期，時間壓在排燈節前的採購季，每年由平台自行公告，通常落在九月下旬到十月上旬。印度真正特別的地方不在折扣，而在它是七個市場裡唯一為「介面」立規的：中央消費者保護局於二〇二三年十一月三十日發布《防止與規範黑暗模式指引》，點名電子商務領域十三種黑暗模式，官方文件舉出的例子包括逐步揭露費用（drip pricing）、偽裝成內容的廣告，以及假造的急迫感。它的上位法是二〇一九年《消費者保護法》，旁邊還有二〇二〇年《消費者保護（電子商務）規則》。二〇二五年五月二十八日，聯邦部長在新德里主持的利害關係人會議把 Amazon、Flipkart、Swiggy、Zomato 等平台找來，要求各家自行稽核並移除黑暗模式。也就是說，別的國家問的是「這個折扣是真的嗎」，印度多問了一句：那個倒數計時器、那個自己跑進購物車的加購品、那筆結帳最後一步才出現的費用，是真的嗎。

## locale en
### title
Shopping Festivals
### summary
Shopping festivals are the one kind of festival the retail trade invented for itself and the state had to regulate afterwards. Federal law in the United States knows Thanksgiving but has never heard of Black Friday. China's market regulator writes to the platforms every year and fixes the reference price for a discount at the lowest transaction price of the previous seven days. Indonesia's Harbolnas carries the trade ministry's name, and the minister himself sells on livestream. India regulates not the discount but the interface. Buy the same thing on the same day and the answer to "can I send it back, for how long, and who pays the postage" is different in every market.
### keywords
Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Singles' Day, Double Eleven, Harbolnas, Big Billion Days, sale season, deceptive pricing, dark patterns, right of withdrawal
### date_rule US black-friday
The day after the fourth Thursday in November, the day after Thanksgiving.
### customs US black-friday
There is no such day as Black Friday in United States federal law. The day with legal standing is the one before it: Thanksgiving Day, listed among the federal holidays at 5 U.S.C. 6103. The Office of Personnel Management's schedules put it on Thursday 26 November 2026, Thursday 25 November 2027 and Thursday 23 November 2028; the Friday that follows is an ordinary working day for federal employees. What does reach the discounts is the Federal Trade Commission's Guides Against Deceptive Pricing, 16 CFR Part 233, published on 8 November 1967 and barely touched since. Section 233.1 says a former price may be used for comparison only if it was "the actual, bona fide price at which the article was offered to the public on a regular basis for a reasonably substantial period of time"; where "an artificial, inflated price was established for the purpose of enabling the subsequent offer of a large reduction", the bargain is a false one. Note the word: these are *guides*, interpretations of section 5 of the FTC Act, and they name no look-back period at all. The Commission's newer fee rule, 16 CFR Part 464, in force since 2025, does require the total price up front — but its definition of a covered good or service reaches only live-event tickets and short-term lodging. Retail goods sit outside it.
### date_rule US cyber-monday
The first Monday after the fourth Thursday in November, the Monday after Thanksgiving.
### customs US cyber-monday
Cyber Monday falls on the Monday after Thanksgiving: 30 November 2026, 29 November 2027, 27 November 2028. It is not a federal holiday either, and no federal regulation has ever been written for it — the name began as a trade-association marketing coinage. Even the statistics are absent: the Census Bureau publishes retail e-commerce quarterly and has never issued a single-day figure, so every record-breaking Cyber Monday number in the press is a private estimate rather than an official measurement. Where the government does appear on that day, it appears as law enforcement. The FBI's Internet Crime Complaint Center issues a holiday shopping alert each year before Thanksgiving; the one dated 15 November 2023 (I-111523-PSA) reported that in the 2022 holiday season alone almost 12,000 victims filed non-payment or non-delivery complaints, with losses of more than $73 million.
### date_rule BR black-friday
The last Friday in November.
### customs BR black-friday
Brazil's Black Friday falls on the last Friday in November: 27 November 2026, 26 November 2027, 24 November 2028. It is not a holiday of any kind and has no statute of its own; everything rests on the Consumer Defence Code, Law 8.078 of 1990. Article 37 forbids misleading advertising. Article 49 gives anyone who bought away from the premises — online or by telephone — seven days from delivery to withdraw, with no reason required. Article 26 keeps the statutory warranty alive on discounted goods: thirty days for non-durables, ninety for durables. Enforcement belongs to the state and federal-district consumer bodies. In October 2023 the Federal District's Consumer Defence Institute, part of the Justice and Citizenship Secretariat, announced it would step up inspection of the main shopping centres and the largest e-commerce sites, checking whether advertised offers were honoured, whether advertising was misleading, and whether customer service existed; complaints go to ten walk-in posts or to 151@procon.df.gov.br. Its director-general, Marcelo Nascimento, told shoppers to track prices beforehand and keep screenshots of the offers. The same body's 2025 guidance records how the shape has changed: promotions now run across the whole of November and well beyond retail — courses, mentoring, digital products — and are advertised by private individuals and informal traders too. Raising a price the day before in order to cut it on the day is named outright as misleading advertising, and is punishable.
### customs CN double-eleven
Double Eleven falls on 11 November. Retailers invented it in 2009; the regulator now writes to them about it every year. The State Administration for Market Regulation sends the major platforms a compliance notice on concentrated online promotions for Double Eleven; the one published on 10 November 2025 runs to six headings: enforcing the platform's own responsibilities; regulating promotional conduct, where forced exclusivity between platforms and price discrimination against loyal customers are both named and forbidden; regulating pricing conduct, where raising a price before discounting it and making false comparisons are forbidden; regulating livestream selling; tightening advertising review; and resolving consumer disputes, including seven-day no-reason returns. The binding instrument behind the notice is the Interim Provisions on Regulating Promotional Conduct, SAMR Order No. 32, promulgated on 29 October 2020 and in force from 1 December 2020. Article 21 requires a discount to state the price it is measured from; where it does not, the reference is fixed by law as the lowest transaction price the same seller charged at the same place during the seven days before the promotion, or the last transaction price if there were no sales in those seven days. Returns come from Article 25 of the Consumer Rights Protection Law and the Interim Measures on Seven-Day No-Reason Returns for Online Purchases: seven days from receipt, with only four exceptions — made to order, perishable, downloaded or unsealed digital goods, and delivered newspapers and periodicals. Of the seven markets, only China puts a number of days into the definition of a discount.
### date_rule JP black-friday
Late November; each retailer announces its own window, most of them aligned with the Friday after American Thanksgiving.
### customs JP black-friday
Japan's Black Friday was imported by the retail trade. It has no legal standing and no official date: each chain announces its own window in late November, and the windows differ in length. Price claims fall under the Act against Unjustifiable Premiums and Misleading Representations. The guidance titled "Views on unfair price representations under the Act" was issued by the Fair Trade Commission on 30 June 2000, revised on 5 December 2002 and 4 January 2006, and taken over by the Consumer Affairs Agency on 1 April 2016. It allows a past price to serve as the comparison price only where the same item was sold at that price "over a substantial recent period" — a period that need not be continuous, since intermittent sales are assessed as a whole, and where "sold" means offered in the ordinary course of business, with no requirement that anyone actually bought it.
